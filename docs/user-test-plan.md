# Sprint 3 Prototype User Test

Goal: check that drivers can complete the core jobs in the working prototype without help, and capture what to fix before building the backend.

## Setup

- Run the app in Expo Go on a phone (`npx expo start`).
- 4 or more participants who drive in Qatar; at least one who did not see the Sprint 2 wireframes.
- One facilitator (reads tasks, does not help), one note taker.
- About 10 minutes per person. Ask them to think aloud.

## Tasks

| # | Task (read aloud) | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Create an account with your number and add your car." | Registers, enters the code, adds a car, reaches Home |
| 2 | "Your battery is dead at CMU-Q. Get someone to jump start it now." | Battery > Jump Start > car > location > Now > places order |
| 3 | "Follow the technician until they arrive, then call them." | Opens Track Order, sees ETA, taps call |
| 4 | "Your car broke down. Get it towed to the nearest garage and check the price first." | Emergency 24/7 > Towing > picks garage on Review > reads estimate |
| 5 | "Use your free car wash." | Taps the Launch Special banner or Car Wash, sees 0.00 QAR |
| 6 | "Close the app and open it again." | Lands on Home without signing in |

## Questions after the tasks

1. On a scale of 1 to 5, how easy was it to request help?
2. What, if anything, confused you?
3. Did you trust the price and arrival time shown? Why or why not?
4. Would you use this instead of calling a garage or your insurer? Why?
5. What is missing that you would need in a real breakdown?
